# Social media for scheduled posts

Pictures and videos for Muse Labs' scheduled posts (Buffer needs public links to post them). They're posted publicly anyway. One folder per batch; the site itself never uses this branch, and nothing here deploys.

- `week-1125/`: the week of Nov 25 to Dec 2, 2026 (Social Hub batch week-1125). The three videos carry Jason's own tracks from Muse Jukebox (Callout, Bell Drop, Exhale). The `four-apps-light-*` slides (Nov 25) replace `four-apps-*` for the Friday carousel, so it looks different from the first post. `penguin-callout-sfx.mp4` (Nov 25) is the penguin clip with the app's own penguin sounds (the + button's boing, the pick-up eep, the throw's whee and the landings) mixed over Callout. `looks-3-wrapped-v2.jpg` (Nov 25) replaces `looks-3-wrapped.jpg` in the real-app carousel: Wrapped's "10 titles since November" screen, since the cover's posters hadn't all loaded in the recording. The `-v2` videos (Nov 25) replace the first ones: every song starts on a beat (Bell Drop on the bar before its drop, Callout on bar 7, Exhale on a phrase); the pick-for-me video keeps only frames where every poster loaded; the Wrapped one drops its "14 days to go" screen and holds its cover less. `pick-belldrop-v3.mp4` (Nov 25, later) replaces the pick-for-me video: a fresh recording with every poster drawn (Ask Muse's Spin my Up Next, spun twice), Bell Drop from a downbeat with its drop on the first spin and both landings on the beat, and the app's own wheel ticks, landing chime and pops; it keeps the first cut's cover and end card.

- `ads/pick-for-me-ad.mp4` (Nov 25): the Meta-ad cut of the pick-for-me video for the App Installs campaign (Dec 1 to 14), 13.4 s: the cover, Ask Muse's Spin my Up Next landing on Spirited Away, then the seamless end card from `ads/penguin-reel-ad.mp4` (just "free on the App Store", no "link in bio"). Bell Drop starts on its drop, the chip tap lands on a downbeat and the landing on the beat, with the app's own wheel ticks and landing chime. Nothing dated.
