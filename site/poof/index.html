How it works

  1. Finds your screenshots. Straight from Windows, including the folder OneDrive quietly moved.
  2. Checks what you pasted. Reads the history Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and Cursor keep on your computer, and recognises your screenshots in it, even resized.
  3. You decide. Everything it found is laid out by day. Star what you want to keep, then poof the rest to the Recycle Bin.

Knows what you pasted

Matches by picture, not just file name, so screenshots pasted straight from the clipboard count too.

Nothing leaves your device

No uploads, no account, no tracking. All the matching happens on your computer.

Clears what AI tools keep

Claude Code keeps a copy of everything you send it, phone screenshots and recordings too. Poof clears those copies as well.

Screen recordings too

Snipping Tool and Game Bar recordings show up next to your screenshots, and play right inside Poof.

Undo built in

Desktop sweeps go to the Recycle Bin. On iPhone, poofed screenshots wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Set and forget

With Pro, screenshots you pasted into AI are cleared automatically each time Poof starts, once they're a few days old.

On your phone, too

Clear what you just took, or anything older or newer than a date you pick. Poof can flag the screenshots you send to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini as you leave the app, and with Pro it reads your screenshots on the phone to find every AI chat, bit of code and error message, from any time. Questions

Does Poof upload my screenshots or chats?

No. Poof reads files on your device and keeps everything there. On Windows the only thing it ever sends is your license key, to check it.

Which AI tools does it know about?

On Windows: Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot and Cursor, including images you pasted from the clipboard. It also checks VS Code and Windsurf chat history for screenshot files you attached. Chats in a web browser, like ChatGPT in Chrome, keep their history online where Poof can't see it; the "Older than" view takes care of those.

Why can't the phone app see what I pasted into ChatGPT?

iOS and Android don't let one app see what you shared into another. That's good for your privacy, so on phones Poof goes by age instead.

What if I delete something I needed?

Nothing is deleted until you press Poof. On Windows it goes to the Recycle Bin; on iPhone it waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Star a screenshot and Poof never suggests it again.

How does Pro work on Windows?

Buy Pro and you get a license key by email. Paste it into Settings → Poof Pro. One key works on up to 3 of your computers.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Poof Pro isn't for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund. Purchases made on a phone are refunded through Apple or Google.

Is there a Mac version?

Windows comes first. A Mac version is planned.
