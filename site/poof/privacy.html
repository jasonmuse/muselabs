Poof

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Poof is made by Jason Muse. It is built to work entirely on your own devices. This page explains what Poof looks at, what it keeps, and the few things that ever leave your device.

The short version

  • Poof never uploads your screenshots, photos or AI chat history.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in Poof.
  • The only things sent anywhere are what's needed to sell Poof Pro and check that you have it, a check for app updates on phones, and, if you turn on Phone link, end-to-end encrypted fingerprints that only your own phone can read.

Poof for Windows and Mac

What it reads on your computer

  • Image and video files in your screenshot folders: the system's screenshots and screen recordings folders, and any folder you add.
  • The history that AI coding tools keep on your computer (Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf), to recognise screenshots you pasted into them.
  • The copies those tools keep of what you send them (Claude Code's uploads folder, and the pictures Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf save from chats), to show them to you. Poof moves them to the Recycle Bin or Trash only when you choose to clear them.
  • While Phone link is on, the list of files inside recent zip files in your Downloads folder, to spot a ChatGPT or Claude data export you downloaded. It reads only exports, and from them only the pictures you sent and when you sent them, to make fingerprints. It never changes or uploads the zip.

What it keeps on your computer

Small thumbnails of your screenshots, screen recordings and those copies, 32×32 grayscale fingerprints used for matching (kept for a year for copies you clear, so the screenshots they match still count as pasted into AI), your settings, your kept list and your license details. They're stored in Poof's own folder (%APPDATA%\Poof on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Poof on Mac), which you can delete at any time.

What leaves your computer

When you activate Poof Pro, Poof sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, so you can see which computers use your key. It re-checks the key every few days. Nothing else is sent.

When you buy Poof Pro, checkout happens on Lemon Squeezy's website. They process your payment and send us your name, email address and order details so we can provide your license and support. Their own privacy policy covers the payment.

Phone link (optional)

Phone link lets Poof on your phone clear the screenshots you sent to AI tools on your computer. It is off until you link a phone: scan the pairing code with it, or tap Find my computer on the phone and click Allow on the computer. While it's on:

  • Poof on your computer sends a list of the images and screen recordings you sent your AI tools, as far back as those tools keep your chats, and the pictures in a ChatGPT or Claude data export you downloaded: for each image a 32×32 grayscale fingerprint, for each recording its shape and length, which AI tool it went to, and when, plus your computer's name. Never the images, the recordings, your screenshots or your chats.
  • The list is encrypted on your computer (AES-256-GCM) with a key that exists only on your computer and the phones you paired, inside the pairing code. Our relay server, run on Cloudflare at link.muselabs.app, only ever stores and passes on data it cannot decrypt, and deletes it 30 days after its last update or as soon as you unlink.
  • Your phone only downloads that list. Apart from a one-time key when you use Find my computer, nothing from your phone is uploaded.
  • Find my computer: while Poof runs on your computer, it keeps a connection open to the relay so a phone on the same network can find it. The relay groups devices by a hash of their network address (your public IP address, or the first half of an IPv6 address), never stores the address itself, and forgets a phone's request after three minutes. The pairing key travels encrypted to a one-time key that only that phone holds (X25519 and AES-256-GCM), so the relay can't read it, and nothing is shared until you click Allow on the computer.
  • Like any website, Cloudflare sees the IP addresses of the devices that connect to the relay.

Poof for iPhone and Android

  • Photos: Poof reads your photo library on your phone to find screenshots and screen recordings and show them to you. With Poof Pro, Swipe and Duplicates go through the rest of your photos and videos too, also only on your phone. Your photos and videos never leave your phone. Deleting always goes through your phone's own confirmation; on iPhone, anything deleted stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Kept on your phone: your kept list, your settings, how many screenshots you've poofed and, with Phone link, fingerprints of your screenshots for matching. With Pro, also the text read from your screenshots (for search, and to find the ones showing an AI chat, code or an error), a tiny grayscale print and the size of each photo (for finding duplicates), and what you've sorted in Swipe.
  • Auto-detect (optional): if you set up the Shortcuts automation, Poof's action runs on your phone when you close the AI apps you picked, and looks at the screenshots and screen recordings you took in the previous half hour. It keeps a list of the ones it has offered to clear, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • ChatGPT and Claude data exports (optional): if you choose your export in Poof or open it in Poof, Poof reads it on your phone: who sent each picture and when, and fingerprints of the pictures you sent, to compare with your screenshots and photos. The export is never uploaded, and Poof deletes its copy once read. It keeps a list of the matching photos on your phone.
  • Send to AI: when you share a screenshot or photo from Poof, your phone tells Poof which app received it, and Poof notes that on your phone. The picture goes only to the app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.
  • App updates: when Poof starts, it asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) whether there's a newer version of the app. The request carries the app's version details, a random ID made on your phone for this installation, and your IP address, which Expo keeps in its request logs. It carries nothing about your photos.
  • Notifications: Poof's notifications are created on your phone. No push service is involved.
  • Purchases: Apple or Google handle payment. Poof uses RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created on your phone, your Poof purchase history, and basic technical details such as app version, system version and country. Poof never sends them your name, email or photos.

Children

Poof is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off Poof's photo access in your phone's settings, delete Poof's data on your computer, or deactivate your license in Settings → Poof Pro. To have your purchase details deleted, email us.
